Professional Summary

Mechanical Design Engineer with 3+ years of experience in MV (IEC 62271) and LV (IEC 61439) electrical panel engineering, from concept development to production-ready drawings. R&D experience on 11 kV, 22 kV and 33 kV Air Insulated Switchgear (AIS) panels for indoor and outdoor installation, including rack-in/rack-out mechanisms, automatic shutter assemblies, and clearance-compliant placement of control transformers (CTs) and potential transformers (PTs).

Design experience across TI, ENERSYS, Siemens 8PU SIEPAN, SIEPAN ELITE and Siemens IPAN panels, with focus on short-circuit withstand capability, IP protection (IEC 60529), internal arc-fault containment (IEC 62271-200 for MV, IEC TR 61641 for LV) and seismic withstand (IS 1893), External Mechanical Impact (IEC 62262). Strong expertise in sheet metal design, GD&T, weldment design, tolerance stack-up analysis, forming allowances and DFM/DFA practices. Experienced in busbar mechanical strength assessment, thermal performance and heat dissipation analysis as per IEC 61439-1 and IEC TR 60890 (airflow optimization, CFM calculation, VFD panel ventilation), enclosure structural validation, and creepage and clearance optimization. Well-versed in Form of Separation (Form 1 to Form 4). Proficient in SolidWorks, Solid Edge and AutoCAD (2D/3D), with parametric 3D modeling, FEA-supported design refinement, detailed 2D drafting and BOM generation.
