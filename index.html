SUB PANELS

This project covers the mechanical and electrical design of an industrial-grade Sub-Panel. Acting as an intermediary in the power distribution chain, this panel takes bulk feeds from the Main Distribution Board (MDB) or Power Control Center (PCC) and distributes them locally to specific factory sections, machinery grids, or utilities. It is designed to house local overcurrent, short-circuit, and earth leakage protection devices for multiple branch circuits, ensuring that faults are isolated close to the source without disrupting the entire facility.
